Good to know

Questions, answered

When do I drop off animals?

Poultry: Fridays 7am to 10am, by appointment. Red meat: Tuesday 11am to 6pm or Wednesday 7am to 9am, with all animals tagged.

Do you deliver?

Yes, in our own refrigerated vans. Free within 10 km, with delivery across Quinte, Kingston, Peterborough and much of the GTA. Check availability at checkout, or choose local pickup.

How long does fresh meat keep?

Fridge (4°C or colder): ground meat 1 to 2 days, whole cuts 3 to 5 days, poultry 2 to 3 days. Freezer: most cuts 3 to 6 months, ground meat 2 to 3 months. Thaw in the fridge.

What temperature should I cook to?

Ground beef and pork 71°C (160°F). Whole poultry 85°C (185°F). Beef or pork steaks and roasts 63°C (145°F) with a 3-minute rest.

What do Canadian beef grades mean?

Prime and AAA have the most marbling for tenderness and flavour. AA and A are leaner and suit stews and slow cooking.

Can I refreeze thawed meat?

Only if it was thawed in the fridge. Meat thawed on the counter should be cooked right away.
